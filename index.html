ABOUT

Remote sensing, GeoAI, and urban environmental change

I am a geospatial scientist specializing in remote sensing, machine learning, and urban geography. My work integrates satellite observations and GeoAI to understand urban expansion, land-use change, microclimates, and sustainability.

My research spans West Africa and South Asia, focusing on impervious surface mapping, vegetation fragmentation, and hourly LST modeling.
